In ReAct, the observation space is augmented by incorporating a language space, allowing the agent to generate thoughts or reasoning traces that do not affect the external environment but help in updating the context for future reasoning or actions. In contrast, Reflexion involves an iterative process where the agent uses short-term and long-term memory to adapt its decision-making based on past experiences and feedback signals. This process allows the agent to improve its decision-making ability by leveraging informative feedback.
